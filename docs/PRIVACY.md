# SmartCity — Privacy Policy

> Phase 0 skeleton. Expanded in Phase 7.

## Data We Collect (opt-in only)

When you explicitly consent on the consent dialog, SmartCity may collect:
- **Anonymous location samples**: latitude, longitude, speed, heading.
- **Session ID**: a random identifier generated per browser session — not linked to any account or identity.

## What We Do NOT Collect

- Name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.
- Precise device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.).
- Your browsing history or behaviour outside SmartCity.

## How Data Is Used

Anonymous location samples are used to estimate real-time edge travel speeds on the local road network. They are never shown to other users and are never sold or shared with third parties.

## Retention

Raw location samples are kept **in memory only** and automatically discarded after **2 minutes**. They are never written to disk, never logged, and not persisted across server restarts.

## Consent and Withdrawal

You can withdraw consent at any time by tapping the "sharing" indicator in the app. Once withdrawn, no further location samples are sent.
