Privacy Policy

Last updated: Phase 7 (date TBD)

Data We Collect (opt-in only)

When you explicitly consent, SmartCity may collect anonymous location samples (latitude, longitude, speed, heading) and a random per-session ID. No accounts, no names, no email addresses.

How Data Is Used

Location samples estimate real-time road speeds. They are never shown to other users, never sold, and never shared with third parties.

Retention

Raw samples are kept in memory only and discarded after 2 minutes. Nothing is written to disk or persisted across server restarts.

Consent & Withdrawal

Tap the "sharing" indicator in the app to stop sharing at any time. Refreshing the page also stops sharing immediately.

Third-Party Services

  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles (MIT)
  • Open-Meteo — weather data (CC BY 4.0, no key)
  • TomTom Traffic Flow — optional, requires API key, disabled by default
